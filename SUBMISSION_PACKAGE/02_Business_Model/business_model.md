# Mo hinh nghiep vu Dealzy

## Muc tieu

Dealzy la he thong thuong mai dien tu ban voucher giam gia truc tuyen, ket noi khach hang voi cac doi tac cung cap dich vu/an uong/giai tri/mua sam. He thong ho tro ba vai tro chinh: Khach hang, Doi tac va Quan tri vien.

## Tac nhan

- Khach hang: dang ky, dang nhap, tim kiem voucher, xem chi tiet voucher, them vao gio hang va quan ly ho so.
- Doi tac: dang ky doanh nghiep, khai bao chi nhanh, tao voucher, gui duyet, quan ly voucher, xem bao cao va xac thuc voucher code.
- Quan tri vien: duyet doi tac, quan ly nguoi dung, duyet/tam an/tu choi voucher, xem dashboard tong quan.

## Gia tri nghiep vu

- Khach hang tiep can uu dai ro rang ve gia, dieu kien ap dung va chi nhanh su dung.
- Doi tac co cong cu dang voucher va theo doi hieu qua chuong trinh.
- Quan tri vien co kha nang kiem soat chat luong doi tac, voucher va vong doi hien thi tren san.

## Pham vi da cai dat

- Xac thuc va quan ly ho so theo vai tro.
- Danh muc, tim kiem va chi tiet voucher.
- Gio hang phia khach hang.
- Quan ly doi tac va chi nhanh.
- Partner dashboard, tao/cap nhat/gui duyet/tam ngung voucher.
- Admin user management, partner approval, voucher approval va dashboard.
- CSDL PostgreSQL co bang Users, Customers, Partners, Branches, Categories, Vouchers, Voucher_Branches, Orders, Order_Items, E_Vouchers, Reviews, Complaints va System_Logs.

## Pham vi con lai

- Tao don hang thuc su tu gio hang.
- Thanh toan mo phong tren API/UI.
- Phat hanh voucher code sau khi thanh toan.
- Lich su don hang va voucher da mua cho khach hang.
- Admin quan ly don hang va nhat ky he thong day du.
